Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Emilio Crespi, Italy, acting as an individual for the Gregory pilot. Contact: emilio@emiliocrespi.it.

2. What Gregory does

Gregory is a coaching service for amateur cyclists. You add it as a connector to your AI assistant (Claude): it reads activities from the services you connect, builds your cyclist profile and answers your questions about your training and that day's nutrition targets. Access is by invitation.

3. What data we process

  • Activity data from the services you connect (for example Garmin Connect, intervals.icu): duration, distance, elevation, speed, power, heart rate, cadence and activity files (FIT).
  • Daily health and wellness data from Garmin Connect, if you allow it: sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, stress, body battery, steps, body weight and composition, fitness metrics such as VO2max.
  • Data you give us: goals, availability, training plan, food rules and preferences, body weight.
  • Connection data: your Garmin user identifier and Garmin access tokens (stored encrypted), connector access tokens (stored only in non-reversible form).
  • Garmin personal key, if you choose to import your history: we use it once to download your past activities and do not store it. We recommend revoking it on Garmin once the import is done.

Heart rate, sleep, heart rate variability, body weight and food data can be health data under Article 9 GDPR. We process them only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Why, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the coaching service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for health data, on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure and working (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with other users. Your data is not used to train AI models.

5. Who else processes it

  • Hosting: a cloud provider with servers in the European Union.
  • Claude (Anthropic PBC, USA): the assistant you chose to connect to Gregory. It receives your data only when you ask it about your training or profile. Claude's processing follows the terms and settings of your Anthropic account.

Data you share from Garmin Connect is sent to Gregory, not to Garmin. Garmin's own processing is described in the Garmin Connect privacy policy.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Where a provider processes data outside the EU (for example Anthropic in the USA), the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

While your account is active, and for up to 30 days after you disconnect or ask for deletion, unless you have explicitly agreed to a longer period. Each source service's own terms may require shorter retention, and we follow them.

8. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to emilio@emiliocrespi.it. You can disconnect any service from its own settings (for example Garmin Connect) or by asking us. You can also ask Claude "delete my Gregory data": we delete everything immediately and remove the Garmin connection. You can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you before the change applies.
