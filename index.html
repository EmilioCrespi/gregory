Gregory

A coach for amateur cyclists that reads your rides, remembers your plan and your rules, and messages you after every workout.

How it works

  1. You connect the services where your rides already are (for example Garmin Connect or intervals.icu).
  2. When you finish and sync a ride, Gregory receives the activity summary.
  3. It compares the ride with your training plan, updates that day's energy and carbohydrate targets, and sends you a short message.
  4. Any change to your plan is only a proposal: nothing changes until you approve it.

Your data

Your data is used only to coach you. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with other users. You can disconnect any service and delete your data at any time. Read the privacy policy (italiano).

Status

Private pilot with a small group of invited cyclists in Italy. Not open to the public yet.
